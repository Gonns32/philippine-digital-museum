How to explore

  • W A S D or arrow keys walk (hold Shift to hurry).
  • Drag the mouse or your finger to look around. On a phone, hold the lower left to walk.
  • Click a frame, or aim at it and press E, to read its story.
  • The Grand Master Hall has four doors: West Past, East Present, North Future, South Game room.
  • Each wing has six rooms: Social, Economic, Political, Cultural, Environmental, Others.
  • R returns you to the Grand Hall. M opens the map. Esc closes any panel. Use Motion to pause all ambient animations. Open Play for the quiz, picture pairs, and timeline challenge. The future wing has three glowing mystery artifacts: aim and press E to investigate.
