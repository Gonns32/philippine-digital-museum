Single-picture walls are centered. The central path is open, with fewer repetitive rugs, benches and overhead strips. Controls: WASD/arrows walk, drag to look, click/E inspect, M map, R lobby, Esc close panels. Bottom controls jump rooms. Motion pauses ambient animations and honors reduced-motion preferences. The arcade includes Then or Now, Picture Pairs, and Timeline Challenge. Choose Play or interact with a cabinet. Discover three glowing mystery artifacts in the Future Political, Environmental and Others rooms with E or a click. On phones, left side moves and right side looks.
